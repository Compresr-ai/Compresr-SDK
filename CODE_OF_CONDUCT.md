# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text is available at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Summary

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@compresr.ai**. All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy and safety of the reporter is the priority.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will follow the Community Impact Guidelines outlined in Contributor Covenant 2.1 when determining consequences for any action they deem in violation. Consequences range from a private warning to permanent removal from the project's community spaces.
